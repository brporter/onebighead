Animation Approach

Click the filter buttons above to see the effect. Non-matching items fade out and scale down (0.25s ease). The grid reflows naturally as items are removed from layout. Short and fluid — no jank.

Note: In production, we'd use CSS view-transitions or animate grid positions for even smoother reflow. This demo shows the core feel.
