C: Asymmetric Gallery Wall

Items arranged in deliberate clusters — large pieces anchored by smaller ones, like art on a gallery wall. Multiple items can be highlighted at different scales. Energy comes from the visual rhythm of large/small alternation across sections.

Note: The colored top-ribbons on featured cards use collection/category accent colors from the Living Gallery palette. All three layouts support featuring items — they differ in how the energy flows: organic discovery (A), bold hero focus (B), or curated wall rhythm (C).
